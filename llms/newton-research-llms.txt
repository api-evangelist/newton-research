# Newton Research

> Newton Research builds a team of specially trained AI agents for marketing analytics. Pre-built or custom agents connect to a customer's existing data infrastructure, write and execute code, and automate analytics workflows — data preparation, audience management, Customer 360, planning and activation, measurement, anomaly detection, and reporting — with data remaining on the customer's own instance.

Sector: AI/ML, marketing analytics (MarTech / AdTech). Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners. Founded by John Hoctor (CEO), Matthew Emans (CTO), and Steven Bennett (CDS). Member of the NVIDIA Inception program. Stated compliance: SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA.
