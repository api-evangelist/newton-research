# Newton Research

> Newton Research builds a team of specially trained AI agents for marketing and advertising analytics. Pre-built or custom agents connect to a customer's existing data infrastructure, write and execute code, and automate analytics workflows — data exploration and preparation, audience management, Customer 360, planning and activation, cross-channel measurement and attribution, anomaly detection, yield management, and automated reporting — with data remaining on the customer's own instance.

Sector: AI/ML, marketing and advertising analytics (MarTech / AdTech). Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners; announced a $9M Series A. Founded by John Hoctor (CEO), Matthew Emans (CTO), and Steven Bennett (CDS). Member of the NVIDIA Inception program. Stated compliance: SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA.

## Developer surface: none published

Newton Research does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, package, CLI, or developer documentation portal as of 2026-08-12. Verified by probe:

- No `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`, `app.`, `mcp.`, `agent.`, `platform.` or `portal.` subdomain resolves (NXDOMAIN). Only `demo.` / `demo2.` resolve, to an AWS ALB currently returning HTTP 503.
- All 18 `/.well-known/` probes across `www.newtonresearch.ai` and `newtonresearch.ai` return 404 — including `security.txt`, `openid-configuration`, `oauth-authorization-server`, `api-catalog`, `ai-plugin.json`, `agent-card.json`, `agent.json` and `adagents.json`.
- `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/pricing` and `/status` all return 404 on the company host.
- No first-party package exists on npm, PyPI, or any other registry. The GitHub organizations named `newtonresearch` and `newtonresearchlabs` belong to *different* companies (newtonresearch.org, an Apple Newton platform project, and newtonlabs.com, a robotics firm) — neither is this Newton Research.

Newton states in its CEO letter on the Ad Context Protocol that the company "leverages MCP (Model Context Protocol) and A2A (agent to agent protocol) today" and endorses AdCP. Those are stated capabilities, not discoverable endpoints: no MCP server URL, agent card, or `adagents.json` is served from any host Newton controls. Access is enterprise sales-gated — the only route is the contact form.
